Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2, 2026

Connections Helper ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information in our mobile application.

1. Data Collection

Connections Helper does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The App uses on-device Machine Learning (ML Kit) to scan screenshots of the "Connections" game. All image processing occurs entirely on your device. We do not upload your screenshots, extracted text, or game state to any external servers.

2. Device Permissions

The App requests the following permissions to function:

  • Screen Capture: Used to take a one-shot screenshot of the "Connections" game to extract the words. This occurs only when you tap the "Scan Screen" button.
  • Overlay (Display over other apps): Used to provide a convenient "Scan" button while you are in the game.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not contain advertisements and utilizes no third-party tracking or analytics services.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
